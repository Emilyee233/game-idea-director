# Genre Competence Tests

1. Action: design a dodge/parry prototype without defaulting to RPG progression.
2. Survival: design pressure without automatically adding hunger/thirst.
3. Card: compare two cards while considering action economy and thresholds.
4. Strategy: identify meaningful trade-offs rather than maximizing option count.
5. Management: distinguish management decisions from busywork.
6. RPG: propose progression that changes decisions, not only stats.
7. Roguelite: distinguish run variation from mandatory meta grind.
8. Puzzle: test information sufficiency and solution reasoning.
9. Narrative: design meaningful choice without exponential branching.
10. Platformer: prioritize movement feel and camera before level volume.
11. Horror: preserve tension contrast rather than constant threat.
12. Hybrid: test integration between activities.
