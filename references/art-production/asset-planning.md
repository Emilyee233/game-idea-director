# Asset Planning

## Purpose
Derive asset needs from gameplay, systems, content, and presentation instead of inventing a decorative shopping list.

## Derivation
For each mechanic/system ask:
- What must the player see, hear, distinguish, manipulate, or understand?
- What states or variants must be represented?
- What feedback is required?
- What content instances are needed for the prototype versus later production?

## Classify assets
- Required for the current milestone.
- Reusable/shared.
- Placeholder-acceptable.
- Procedurally generated or data-driven.
- Deferred expansion.
- Optional polish.

## Cost drivers
Count more than asset quantity. Consider:
- Number of states/variants.
- Animation.
- Layering and occlusion.
- Resolution and camera distance.
- Rigging or collision.
- Localization/text.
- Engine setup.
- QA and revision.

## Priority
Prioritize assets that unblock core gameplay and validation. Do not produce a large final-quality library before the relevant gameplay is proven.

## Output
When useful, create an asset registry containing ID, purpose, dependency, priority, status, specification link, and acceptance criteria.
