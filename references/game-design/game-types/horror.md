# Horror Design

Use when fear, dread, vulnerability, uncertainty, pursuit, or disturbing discovery is central.

## Design dimensions
Threat source; information control; agency versus vulnerability; resource pressure if relevant; safe spaces and tension release; audio/visibility/pacing; failure and repetition.

## Common failures
Constant threat removes contrast; repeated scares become predictable; scarcity creates annoyance; AI becomes obviously exploitable; retries reveal the machinery; darkness prevents play rather than creates uncertainty.

## Prototype
Test a short tension arc. Observe anticipation, caution, comprehension, and recovery. Do not infer fear from atmosphere descriptions alone.
