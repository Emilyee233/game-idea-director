# Narrative Game Design

Use when story discovery, relationships, dialogue, choices, interpretation, or authored consequences are central.

## Design dimensions
What the player does to engage with story; information discovered through play; what choices express; consequence timing/visibility; remembered state; pacing interaction with gameplay.

A choice can be meaningful through consequence, self-expression, information, relationship change, resource trade-off, or commitment. Not every choice needs a branching ending.

## Common failures
Choices pretend to matter but converge invisibly; branching grows exponentially without equivalent value; story pauses gameplay rather than integrating with it; narrative state becomes inconsistent.

## Prototype
Test one short situation with a small number of meaningful states and consequences before scaling branches.
