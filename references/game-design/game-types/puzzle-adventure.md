# Puzzle & Adventure Design

Use when observation, inference, rule discovery, spatial reasoning, experimentation, or information progression is central.

## Design dimensions
Required rule/relationship; information availability; safe experimentation; feedback; concept introduction/combination; solution space; reset cost; hint structure.

## Common failures
Missing information prevents deduction; solutions rely on arbitrary convention; difficulty becomes tedious execution after insight; hints reveal answers rather than restore reasoning; one obscure interaction blocks progression.

## Prototype
Test with players who do not know the solution. Observe their mental models, incorrect hypotheses, and whether feedback helps them recover.
