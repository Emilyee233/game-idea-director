# RPG & Character-Build Design

Do not assume an RPG needs levels, classes, loot rarity, quest logs, dialogue trees, or a large world.

## Design dimensions
Character identity; growth; build expression; combat/non-combat resolution; equipment/skill interactions; exploration; objectives; choice/consequence or relationships where relevant.

## Progression test
Ask whether a reward changes what the player can do, what decisions are available, or how problems can be approached—or merely increases numbers.

## Common failures
Stat inflation substitutes for decisions; loot quantity overwhelms evaluation; builds converge on one optimum; narrative choices are cosmetic; content burden expands faster than systemic depth.

## Prototype
Test one representative progression choice and a challenge that can be approached differently because of it.
