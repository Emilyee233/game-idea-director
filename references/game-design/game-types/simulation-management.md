# Simulation & Management Design

Use when players manage flows, services, production, facilities, populations, logistics, or economic trade-offs.

## Design dimensions
Inputs, transformations, outputs, bottlenecks, capacity, costs, revenues, demand, service quality, scheduling, spatial layout, feedback delays, growth, specialization, and player control versus autonomous simulation.

## Meaningful-management test
Ask what decisions produce different operational outcomes. Repeated clicking, restocking, or servicing is not automatically management gameplay.

## Common failures
One obvious upgrade path; busywork replaces planning; profit is guaranteed regardless of decisions; growth increases quantities but not decision complexity; simulation detail does not affect choices.

## Prototype
Model a small operational loop with a few interacting resources and at least two viable strategies. Validate cause-and-effect readability before adding content volume.
