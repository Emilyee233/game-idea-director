# Stealth Design

Use when avoiding detection, manipulating awareness, gathering information, or controlled exposure is central.

## Design dimensions
Sight/sound/suspicion/memory; player knowledge of enemy perception; patrol/search behavior; concealment/distraction/timing; recovery after partial detection; route alternatives.

## Common failures
Detection is inconsistent; failure immediately resets rather than creates recovery; one distraction solves everything; search behavior feels arbitrary; stealth becomes slow waiting.

## Prototype
Test one encounter with readable detection, at least two viable approaches, and a recoverable alert state.
