# Strategy & Tactics Design

Use when planning, allocation, positioning, forecasting, information, or controlling multiple units/resources is central.

## Design dimensions
Decision horizon; information quality; resources and opportunity cost; position/territory/range; unit differentiation and counterplay; action order; strategic commitment; opponent modeling.

A strategic choice should normally involve a meaningful trade-off, uncertainty, timing, or opportunity cost. More options do not automatically create more depth.

## Common failures
One dominant strategy; hard counters decide outcomes before play; information overload creates analysis paralysis; units differ only in stats; AI difficulty relies mostly on bonuses.

## Prototype
Use a small ruleset and compact decision space. Look for multiple viable plans and understandable counterplay before expanding factions, tech trees, or maps.
