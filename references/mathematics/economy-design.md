# Game Economy Design

For each important resource define sources, sinks, storage/capacity, conversions, timing, scarcity, loss/decay if any, and player decisions affected.

Track rates as well as totals: income, mandatory/optional spending, production/consumption, bottlenecks, reserves, and recovery after mistakes.

Ask what scarcity makes the player decide, whether resources accumulate without use, whether negative loops become unrecoverable, whether one resource dominates, and whether costs support intended progression.

Common failures include infinite profit loops, mandatory costs exceeding reliable income, inflation, redundant currencies, rising prices without new decisions, and punishing experimentation.

Use tables or simulation when several rates interact. Mathematical stability does not prove the economy feels engaging or fair.
