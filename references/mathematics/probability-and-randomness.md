# Probability and Randomness

Use randomness intentionally: variety, uncertainty, adaptation, risk management, discovery, fairness, or recombination. If it serves no clear purpose, reconsider it.

As relevant calculate event probability, complements, expected value, variance/spread, repeated trials, conditional probability, draws without replacement, and outcome distributions.

Ask how players can predict, mitigate, reroll, hedge, build for consistency, or recover.

For decks, draw probability depends on deck size, copies, cards seen, draw count, reshuffle, tutoring, discard/exhaust, and mulligans.

Expected value can hide extreme variance. For interacting systems, simulate distributions and compare mathematics with player perception.
