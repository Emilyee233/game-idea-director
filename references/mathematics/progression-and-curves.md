# Progression and Curves

Distinguish numerical power, new actions/mechanics, strategic options, content access, knowledge/mastery, collection, and narrative/relationship progression. Do not assume progression means larger stats.

Ask what changes over time, what remains stable, which milestones matter, how quickly complexity appears, and whether earlier options are invalidated too quickly.

Linear, polynomial, exponential, logarithmic, stepped, capped, and custom curves are tools, not defaults. Choose based on intended pacing.

Identify breakpoints where small numeric changes cause large experiential changes: hits to defeat, turns/actions required, affordability, capacity, or cooldown windows. Tune around meaningful thresholds, then test actual pacing.
