# Team Roles and Responsibility Design

## Purpose
Map work to responsibilities without mechanically creating one human or AI agent per traditional studio job.

## Responsibilities may include
- Game design.
- Production.
- Narrative.
- Art direction.
- Asset production.
- Technical design.
- Programming.
- Audio.
- QA/playtesting.

One person or agent may cover several responsibilities when scope permits.

## Assignment test
For each responsibility ask:
- What decisions are required?
- What expertise is required?
- What files/systems are touched?
- What evidence constitutes completion?
- Who can approve the result?
- What conflicts could arise with parallel work?

## Human authority
Important creative direction, scope changes, irreversible operations, and final acceptance remain with the authorized human unless explicitly delegated.
