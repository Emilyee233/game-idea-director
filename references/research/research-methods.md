# Research Methods

## Purpose
Research only when a concrete design, technical, production, or market question needs external evidence.

## Workflow
1. Define the question.
2. Define scope, version, platform, and terms.
3. Choose sources appropriate to the claim.
4. Separate observation from interpretation.
5. Cross-check important or disputed claims.
6. Record source, date, uncertainty, and implication.
7. Convert findings into a decision or next test.

Do not browse merely to decorate an answer with citations.
