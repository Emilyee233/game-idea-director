# Research Records

## Purpose
Keep important research auditable and separate facts from interpretation.

## Record
- Research ID.
- Question.
- Scope/version/date.
- Sources.
- Direct observations.
- Verified facts/claims.
- Interpretation.
- Uncertainty/conflicts.
- Design implications.
- Recommendation.
- Follow-up verification.

Research records inform decisions; they do not automatically become confirmed project rules.
