# Source Evaluation

## Purpose
Match source quality to the claim being made.

## Prefer, depending on the question
- Official engine/platform documentation for APIs and requirements.
- Direct gameplay observation for visible mechanics.
- Official game/developer material for stated features and design intent.
- Reproducible projects/tests for technical behavior.
- Multiple independent player reports for experience patterns.
- Community discussion for hypotheses and edge cases, not unquestioned fact.

## Evidence labels
Use practical labels such as:
- Verified/reproducible.
- Strongly supported.
- Single-source.
- Community/experience report.
- Inference/hypothesis.

Do not turn source hierarchy into fake numerical certainty.
