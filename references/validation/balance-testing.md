# Balance Testing

## Purpose
Validate numerical and strategic balance using the appropriate combination of analysis, simulation, telemetry, and playtesting.

## Layers
- Formula/efficiency checks.
- Breakpoint and edge-case checks.
- Strategy/build interaction.
- Simulation across representative states.
- Human play and perceived fairness.

## Watch
- Dominant options.
- Dead options.
- Infinite/compounding loops.
- Unrecoverable states.
- Strategy diversity.
- Pick/use/win rates interpreted in context.
- Skill-level differences.

Do not treat equal efficiency as automatically balanced, or unequal efficiency as automatically unbalanced.
