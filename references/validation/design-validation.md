# Design Validation

## Purpose
Check whether a design is coherent and testable before confusing a well-written specification with a proven experience.

## Review
- Intended player experience is explicit.
- Core actions and decisions support it.
- Rules are understandable.
- Systems have a reason to exist.
- Incentives do not obviously contradict the intended behavior.
- Scope matches the milestone.
- Key assumptions are identified.
- A test can falsify important hypotheses.

## Output
Separate:
- Confirmed design.
- Open question.
- Risk.
- Hypothesis.
- Recommended test.

A design review can justify building a prototype; it cannot prove fun.
