# Playtesting

## Purpose
Observe whether real players understand and experience the game as intended.

## Define the question
A playtest should answer specific questions, not merely ask "is it fun?"

Examples:
- Do players understand the goal without explanation?
- Do they recognize a trade-off?
- Where do they hesitate?
- Why do they fail?
- Do they choose different viable strategies?
- Does the intended emotion appear?

## Observe before explaining
Record behavior, errors, pauses, workarounds, comments, and outcomes. Avoid teaching the solution during the test unless the test explicitly concerns onboarding support.

## Separate
- What the player did.
- What the player said.
- What the observer inferred.

One player's opinion is evidence, not universal truth.
